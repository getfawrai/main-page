    01The leak

    Established clinics don’t have a lead problem.
    They have a follow-up problem.

    The enquiries arrive, and your team replies to most of them. But not fast enough. By the time someone answers, the patient has messaged four or five other clinics, and the ones that replied first already have their attention.

    The first hour is where the booking is won or lost.
